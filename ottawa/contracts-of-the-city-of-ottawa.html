A contract awarded without competition is allowed by the City’s procurement by-law in specific cases and the City writes which section it relied on.

How to read this page

  • The amount is the one the City publishes for the contract or for the change made to it. An amendment or an extension is a new line, with its own amount.
  • The type is the City’s: Initial (a new contract), Amendment, Extension, Follow-On, or a combination.
  • Non-competitive: when the City awarded without a competitive process, it writes the section of its procurement by-law it relied on (for example “Section 22 (1) (J)”) and, often, a reason. Both are shown as written.
  • What is not here: the exact award date (the City gives only the half-year), the companies that bid, contracts under $25,000 and the link between a contract and a Council decision.
