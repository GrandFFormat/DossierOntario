What this site covers

Ottawa City Council is the Mayor, elected city-wide and 24 councillors, one per ward. Most files go first to a standing committee (Planning and Housing, Finance and Corporate Services, Audit, Built Heritage…), then to Council, which makes them final. The City Clerk publishes the minutes of each meeting in English and in French. That is what this site reads.

Each card is one item of the minutes: its title as the City writes it, the result, the motion and, when Council held a recorded vote, who voted for and against. The plain-language summary is written by AI from the official minutes and identified as such on every card. The link to the minutes is always there and the minutes prevail.

The vocabulary (by-law, zoning amendment, delegated authority, in camera) is explained in the glossary. The Sources page says where every number comes from and what a machine can miss.
