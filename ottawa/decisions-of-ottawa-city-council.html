Decisions of Ottawa City Council

Here are the decisions of Ottawa City Council and its standing committees: each card is one item of the minutes: a staff report, a by-law, a zoning amendment, a motion, grouped by meeting. Open a card for its plain-language summary, the exact text from the minutes and, when there was a recorded vote, who voted for and against. The members are on the City Council page.

What a card contains. The title, result and motion of the item, copied from the minutes the City Clerk publishes on eScribe. The summary is written by AI from those minutes and says so; it adds nothing and judges nothing. If it differs from the minutes, the minutes prevail: they are one click away on every card.
