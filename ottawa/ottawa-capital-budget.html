How to read this page

  • The unit. The City’s budget files do not name their unit. Their sum (operating 2026: 5,220,570) matches a budget of about $5.2 billion, so the amounts are read as thousands of dollars. That is our deduction, not an official statement.
  • The operating budget pays for day-to-day services (transit, police, waste, parks, salaries). The capital budget pays for what is built or bought. They are separate.
  • Taxes by ward are the total property taxes the City bills in each ward, with the total assessed value. This page does not estimate your own bill.
