Ottawa City Council

The ward map The members Votes against, by member

Ottawa City Council has 25 members: the Mayor, elected by the whole city and 24 councillors, each elected by one ward, for four years. The names, wards and contact details come from the City’s open data (Elected Officials 2022-2026, Wards 2022-2026). Attendance comes from the City’s data set of Council meeting attendance. Their decisions are on the Decisions page.
