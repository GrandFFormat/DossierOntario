Bill 13

Primary Care Act, 2025

Sponsored by Jones, Hon. Sylvia · Royal Assent received. Statutes of Ontario 2025, chapter 8

  • Creates the Primary Care Act, 2025, setting Ontario's vision for publicly funded primary care
  • Sets six objectives: province-wide access, connected services, convenient timing, inclusive and non-discriminatory care, empowered access to personal health information, and responsive system
  • Requires the Minister to publish an annual report on progress toward these objectives, starting within one year of the Act coming into force
  • Report must include the percentage of insured persons with an ongoing relationship with a primary care clinician or team
  • Clarifies the Act creates no private legal right, duty of care, or fiduciary duty for any person
  • States that failing to comply with the Act does not invalidate any related policy, law, regulation or decision
  • Allows the Lieutenant Governor in Council to make regulations detailing objectives, reporting information and how reports are published
